Messy Credit App to DMN Ruling Engine

Team Abubakar & Martínez · Track 4 · Decision models in an AI brain for industry · Working plan, 14 November 2026 · Facts marked [verified] · [declared] · [unverified]

In one line: messy commercial credit applications go in; the pipeline extracts structured financial inputs, evaluates them against a DMN decision model, and returns an auditable decision object with the rule that fired, or ABSTAIN when mandatory data is missing.

Verdict (self-assessed): BUILD IT · Potential 85 · Readiness 80

Selection criteria (written before the ideas)

  1. Core decision loop demoable end to end today (input situation → constraint evaluation → auditable decision record + rationale).
  2. Clear economic pain (cost of a wrong decision or audit failure > €50k/year [declared]).
  3. Moat beyond a prompt: formal decision object with constraints, alternatives evaluated, and deterministic abstention (refusal when data missing).

Chosen because DMN is the open enterprise decision standard, enabling auditable credit approvals with deterministic abstention.

The canvas

Q1Problem

Commercial loan underwriters and credit risk officers lose 3–5 hours per application [declared] manually cross-referencing messy PDFs, tax filings and bank statements; today they copy-paste into Excel scoring templates with no auditable trace of policy overrides.

Q2Focus

Step 3: application ingestion, policy constraint verification, and credit grant/deny ruling, for commercial B2B lending. Explicitly ignored: KYC identity verification, disbursement payments, loan servicing.

Q3UVP to test

For commercial credit officers who waste hours parsing messy loan packets under regulatory audit pressure, our pipeline extracts structured financial context and produces an executable DMN decision model and an auditable decision object — unlike black-box LLM copilots — because it decouples unstructured extraction from deterministic, standard-compliant decision logic.

Hypothesis: underwriters will trust AI-assisted decisions only if backed by an executable OMG-standard DMN table and deterministic abstention on missing data.

Test today: run 10 messy credit application files through the pipeline; decisions match ground-truth loan policies in >90% of cases.

Kill criterion (ex-ante): the pipeline returns a ruling on missing mandatory financial metrics instead of ABSTAIN in more than 0 test cases. Result: pending.
